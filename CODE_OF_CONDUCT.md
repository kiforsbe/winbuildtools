# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical disagreements with
evidence and focus criticism on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
abuse, disclosure of private information and persistent unwanted contact are
not acceptable in project discussions or reviews.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to keep
the project usable. Report conduct concerns through an established private
maintainer channel. Public hosting must configure and document that channel;
this local setup does not invent contact details or promise response times.
